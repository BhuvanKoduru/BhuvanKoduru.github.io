I'm a Master's student in AI/NLP at Carnegie Mellon University's Language Technologies Institute, graduating in December 2026. I study the gap between what models represent and what they act on, using mechanistic interpretability, activation steering, and large-scale evaluation.

Two examples: audio-language models that encode how something is said but lose it before they respond (CoLM 2026), and computer-use agents whose chat-time safety doesn't carry over to their actions (NeurIPS 2026 workshop).

This summer I was a research intern at Simular.ai, working on self-evolving computer-use agents and agent safety. Before that I built AmongUs-X at CMU's Robotics Institute, a benchmark of 8,000+ Among Us games measuring deception and Theory of Mind in 21 LLMs. Before CMU, I built multi-agent systems at Adobe.

02 Selected Publications

Heard but Not Heeded: Paralinguistic Information Encoding and Loss in Audio-Language Models
B. Koduru, D. Alharthi, R. Singh, B. Raj
CoLM 2026

Speaking style is strongly encoded in the audio encoder of four audio-language models, but degrades before it reaches the output.

arXiv PDF
Chat-safe is not CUA-safe: Chat Methods May Not Transfer to Computer Use
B. Koduru, J. Yang, A. Li, X. E. Wang
NeurIPS 2026 Workshop Agents in the Wild · poster

Refusal drops from 31/47 in chat to 4/47 when the same models act as computer-use agents; activation steering that raises chat refusal leaves agent actions unchanged.

RIVET: Robust Idempotent Voice Attribute Editing
D. Alharthi, B. Koduru, R. Singh, B. Raj
INTERSPEECH 2026 oral

Training voice editors to be idempotent makes them robust to noisy attribute labels, improving edit success and speaker-identity preservation.

arXiv PDF
AmongUs-X: Benchmarking Strategic Deception of LLM Agents via Theory-of-Mind Metrics
K. Mirakhor*, B. Koduru*, A. Kontogiannis, R. Zabounidis, K. Sycara, W. Kim
AAMAS 2027 · under review *equal contribution

8,000+ simulated Among Us games across 21 LLMs, measuring whether agents deceive and how well they model each other's beliefs.
