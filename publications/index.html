  1. CoLM
    Heard but Not Heeded: Paralinguistic Information Encoding and Loss in Audio-Language Models
    Bhuvan Koduru, Dalaver Alharthi, Rita Singh, and 1 more author
    In Conference on Language Modeling (CoLM), 2026
    Abs Bib

    We investigate how audio-language models encode and preserve paralinguistic information such as emotion, prosody, and speaker characteristics across their internal representations. Our findings reveal systematic information loss patterns that impact downstream performance.

  2. ICML MI
    Subliminal Prosody Learning: Auxiliary Emotion Supervision Redistributes Affective Representations Across ALM Layers
    Bhuvan Koduru, Dalaver Alharthi, Rita Singh, and 1 more author
    In ICML Workshop on Mechanistic Interpretability, 2026
    Abs Bib

    Through mechanistic interpretability techniques, we demonstrate how auxiliary emotion supervision reshapes the distribution of affective representations across audio-language model layers, improving emotional understanding without explicit fine-tuning.

  5. NeurIPS (sub)
    AmongUs-X: Benchmarking Strategic Deception of LLM Agents via Theory-of-Mind Metrics
    Kian Mirakhor*, Bhuvan Koduru*, Apostolos Kontogiannis, and 3 more authors
    Under review at NeurIPS, 2026
    *equal contribution
    Abs Bib

    We introduce AmongUs-X, a novel benchmark for evaluating strategic deception and Theory of Mind capabilities in LLM agents through 8,000+ simulated multi-agent games. Our game-theoretic framework reveals fundamental limitations in current LLMs’ ability to model others’ mental states and engage in strategic deception.
