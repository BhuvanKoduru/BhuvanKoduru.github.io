# Teaching

## Machine Learning & Deep Learning Workshop  
**Google Developer Student Clubs – JSSSTU**  
**Dates:** April 10–12, 2023  

👉 Check out my [LinkedIn post about the experience](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bhuvankoduru_machinelearning-deeplearning-googledeveloperstudentclubs-activity-7059970988465127424-Ae-1?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAC7YDrcBFAJOE0d11fK7jszVGDLg4griIrk).  

I was nervous, but I DID IT! Teaching my first ML/DL workshop was an absolute blast.  
From April 10 to 12, my dream—and slight fear—of teaching took center stage as I led a three-day workshop with my fellow club members at Google Developer Student Clubs, JSSSTU.  

I’ll be honest: standing in front of a screen full of eager faces was intimidating, but by Day 3, I was in my element. Cue the endorphins!  

### What We Covered
- The basics of **Machine Learning**  
- What’s inside a **Neuron**, and how **Neural Networks** learn  
- Why **Convolutional Neural Networks** are game-changers  
- And plenty of “Aha!” moments along the way  

### Why It Was Special
- Participants showed up all three days, soaking it all in and asking questions that gave me goosebumps.  
- I felt that mix of *“I can’t believe I’m actually doing this”* and *“Let’s do this again!”* with every answer I shared.  

This experience not only strengthened my technical communication skills but also sparked my passion for teaching. I’m grateful to everyone involved for making this workshop such a success.
